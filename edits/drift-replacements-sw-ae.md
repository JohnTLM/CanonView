# Word replacements: canon drift in Substrateless World and Anatta to Emptiness

These are the same changes already made on the website (live site versions 64 and 66), so the site and the Word manuscripts agree again.
Apply each one in Word with Find and Replace (Ctrl+H). Copy the **Find** text exactly. Each one occurs once in its book.
If Word cannot find a passage, search for its first five words, since a line break or a smart quote may differ.
After applying, the books can be rebuilt from the Word files as usual (prog-spec section 7) without undoing the site changes.

## The Logical Necessity of a Substrateless World

Files: substrateless-world-v67-A4.docx and substrateless-world-v67.docx.

### 1. §13.3

**Find:**

> Appearance becomes luminous and dream-like

**Replace with:**

> Appearance is met as luminous and dream-like

### 2. §13.8

**Find:**

> The constraint that keeps designation from being arbitrary comes from the vividness itself.

**Replace with:**

> The constraint that keeps designation from being arbitrary is not a given layer beneath it: it is the reliability in use of appearances that are vivid and designated together (section 13.2).

### 3. §14.3

**Find:**

> is the power of what is dependent and empty, and therefore free to act.

**Replace with:**

> is the power of what is dependent and empty: nothing in it is fixed, so nothing stops it acting.

### 4. §15.6

**Find:**

> Phenomena can appear in such varied, dynamic and context-dependent ways precisely because they are empty.

**Replace with:**

> Phenomena appear in such varied, dynamic and context-dependent ways because they depend, and their emptiness is not a further reason behind that: it is what their depending amounts to.
