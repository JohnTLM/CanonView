# Word replacements: canon drift in Substrateless World and Anatta to Emptiness

These are the same changes already made on the website (live site version 64), so the site and the Word manuscripts agree again.
Apply each one in Word with Find and Replace (Ctrl+H). Copy the **Find** text exactly. Each one occurs once in its book.
If Word cannot find a passage, search for its first five words, since a line break or a smart quote may differ.
After applying, the books can be rebuilt from the Word files as usual (prog-spec section 7) without undoing the site changes.

## The Logical Necessity of a Substrateless World

Files: substrateless-world-v67-A4.docx and substrateless-world-v67.docx.

### 1. §13.3

**Find:**

> Appearance becomes luminous and dream-like

**Replace with:**

> Appearance is met as luminous and dream-like

### 2. §13.8

**Find:**

> The constraint that keeps designation from being arbitrary comes from the vividness itself.

**Replace with:**

> The constraint that keeps designation from being arbitrary is not a given layer beneath it: it is the reliability in use of appearances that are vivid and designated together (section 13.2).

### 3. §14.3

**Find:**

> is the power of what is dependent and empty, and therefore free to act.

**Replace with:**

> is the power of what is dependent and empty: nothing in it is fixed, so nothing stops it acting.

### 4. §15.6

**Find:**

> Phenomena can appear in such varied, dynamic and context-dependent ways precisely because they are empty.

**Replace with:**

> Phenomena appear in such varied, dynamic and context-dependent ways because they depend, and their emptiness is not a further reason behind that: it is what their depending amounts to.

## The Transition from Anatta to Emptiness

Files: anatta-to-emptiness-v24-A4.docx and anatta-to-emptiness-v24.docx.

### 1. The Taste of Total Exertion, list of tastes

**Find:**

> Luminosity belongs to the event itself.

**Replace with:**

> Luminosity is simply the event appearing, not a light added to it.

### 2. The Taste of Total Exertion, Luminous Clarity Without Background Awareness

**Find:**

> The clarity belongs to the manifesting itself.

**Replace with:**

> The clarity is the manifesting itself, not something it possesses.

### 3. The Last Refuge of Ontology, the final reversal

**Find:**

> Precisely because appearances lack any inherent substrate, they are already primordially open, non-abiding,

**Replace with:**

> Appearances lack any inherent substrate, and, met without fixing, they are seen to be already open, non-abiding,

### 4. Ye Shes

**Find:**

> Freedom belongs to dependent arising itself.

**Replace with:**

> It is not the possession of any mode of cognition: it is dependent arising seen without fixing.

### 5. The Taste, Not the Argument

**Find:**

> Openness is not an attribute belonging to a hidden subject, but the direct consequence of a world devoid of fixed essence.

**Replace with:**

> Openness is not an attribute belonging to a hidden subject. It is how a world devoid of fixed essence is met when nothing in it is being fixed.

### 6. The Taste, Not the Argument

**Find:**

> Radiance is immediate precisely because it is completely empty.

**Replace with:**

> Radiance is immediate, and it is completely empty; neither is the reason for the other.

### 7. A Beginner's Companion, substantialist non-duality

**Find:**

> a non-duality in which even the ground-nature, the luminosity, the awareness itself, is seen as dependently designated rather than independently findable, while remaining vividly present.

**Replace with:**

> a non-duality in which even what was taken as the ground-nature, the luminosity, the awareness itself, is seen as dependently designated rather than independently findable, while appearing remains vividly present.
