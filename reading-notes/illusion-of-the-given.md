# The Illusion of the Given: citation notes

Bonus article *The Illusion of the Given: Madhyamaka, Non-Foundational Dzogchen, and the Dissolution of the Referent*
(v3, 12 pages). Published files: EBooks repo, `intersubjectivity-and-dependent-arising/` (merged in EBooks#11).

This file tracks every citation in the article and its verification status, following the practice of the OP
verification log. **Status** is one of:
- **Confirmed (series)**: already confirmed elsewhere in the series; the source of the confirmation is given.
- **Confirmed (author)**: confirmed by the author from their own reading; recorded with the date.
- **To verify**: not yet checked against a primary text or a reliable translation. Do not cite in a book until checked.

Section numbers are those of v3.
