# The Canon View in Plain English

This is a simple version of the Canon View, the set of positions that all four books and the articles by
John Tan (Thusness) follow. The numbers match the full Canon View (https://claude.ai/artifact/Lqbt2KhCCNsUrxk5HoxfEa), so any point here can be looked up there,
with its quotations and sources. Published as a doc: https://claude.ai/artifact/6BVWBq1XHuvsYydfVyawsE

## The view in one paragraph

Nothing exists by itself. Everything, your own mind included, comes about through other things: causes,
parts, conditions, and the way it is picked out and named. Look for a thing apart from all that, and
nothing is found. This is called *emptiness*. It does not mean things are unreal: they appear clearly and
work reliably. And emptiness is not a hidden something behind them. On the clear, knowing quality of
experience (*luminosity*), the books follow the Dzogchen tradition: that clarity is simply how appearing
shows itself, and it is empty too.

This is the reading of the Indian teacher Nāgārjuna known in Tibet as *rangtong*, "empty of its own
nature". It is not the reading called *shentong*, "empty of other", which keeps one ultimate reality that is
empty of everything else but not of itself.

## A few words used below

- **Empty**: not existing by itself, from its own side. Empty does not mean nothing.
- **Dependent arising**: things come about through causes and conditions, and depend on parts and context.
- **Designated**: picked out as "this" rather than "that", by seeing, thinking or naming.
- **Fixing**: the habit of taking what appears as a solid thing that stands on its own. The books also call
  it *reifying* or *grasping*.
- **Conventional**: how things work in everyday life. It is the only kind of "real" there is, and it is fully
  valid.

## Part One: How things are

1. **One emptiness for everything.** Mind and matter are empty in the same way. There is no special, deeper
   emptiness kept for the mind.

2. **Empty means "not existing by itself", and nothing more.** Emptiness is not a hidden essence, a vast space
   or a pure awareness behind things. "Not found" does not mean "doesn't happen".

3. **Emptiness is empty too.** Emptiness is always the emptiness of something, like a cup not being a cup all by
   itself. It is not a thing behind the cup. Think of it as medicine: it clears the illness of fixing, then
   leaves as well. If you hold on to emptiness as "the truth", the medicine has become a new illness.

4. **The mind gets no exemption.** Look for the mind the way you would look for anything else, and it is not
   found standing on its own either. The mind is not the floor everything else rests on.

5. **The knower and the known come together.** Awareness is always awareness of something, through something.
   There is no inner viewer sitting behind the eyes.

6. **Neither "all is mind" nor "all is matter".** Making the mind the one real thing is the same mistake as
   making matter the one real thing. In a dream, the dreamed mountain and the dreaming mind both vanish on
   waking.

7. **Two truths, one world.** Everyday reality and emptiness are not two levels, one higher and one lower.
   They are two sides of the same appearance. The everyday world is fully valid: it has standards for telling a
   rope from a snake.

8. **Emptiness doesn't cause or explain anything.** Emptiness is just what dependence comes to when you examine
   it. Don't say things can appear "because they are empty".

9. **A name is not a label stuck on a finished thing.** Things are picked out, by seeing, thinking or naming,
   together with their parts and surroundings. The mountain was there before anyone named it, but no mountain
   comes with its own edges drawn by nature. This is not "anything goes": you can't call a shadow a bridge and
   walk across it. Being designated doesn't make something less real. It is how anything is real.

10. **Connections are empty too.** When things are seen to depend on each other, the web of connections does not
    become the new foundation. Neither the things nor their links come first.

11. **Change with no one changing, continuity with nothing carried.** A candle flame at midnight is neither the
    flame at dusk nor a different flame, and nothing travels from one to the other. Continuity is real, but
    nothing passes through it unchanged.

12. **Things work without an inner essence.** A key opens a lock only together with the lock, the hand and the
    turning. Something that existed all by itself couldn't change, combine or act at all.

13. **Empty is not flimsy.** Empty things are vivid and work fully. A car with no essence can still hurt you.
    Grief and healing are real.

14. **Nothing that can be pointed to is an exception.** Whatever can be identified, including "emptiness",
    "the ultimate", "luminosity" and "nirvāṇa", is designated and empty. Nothing secret is left over.

15. **"Beyond words" doesn't mean a hidden something.** When the ultimate is called inexpressible, it means there
    is nothing further to grasp. It does not mean a mysterious reality that words just fail to reach.

16. **Nirvāṇa is this same life, with the fixing ended.** It is not another place, and not something built by
    practice. Nothing is added to the world and nothing is taken away. What changes is how things are held. No
    one owns nirvāṇa.

17. **Things depend on conditions, with no hidden producer.** Causes and conditions really work in everyday life.
    But nothing produces its effect all by itself. In the end, asking "but why does anything appear at all?"
    expects a final ground. When no ground is found, the question simply stops applying.

18. **No-self is real insight, but not the whole view.** Seeing that there is no viewer behind seeing is a
    genuine breakthrough. Nobody disappears: the person carries on, remembers and keeps promises. But the same
    seeing must also reach things, conditions and dependence itself. Otherwise the experience and the view fall out
    of step.

19. **Mental spinning stops when its object isn't found.** The mind spins stories of "it is", "it isn't", "it came",
    "it went" around things it takes to be solid. When those things aren't found, the spinning stops by itself,
    and that stopping is peace. A quiet mind is not the same as wisdom if the habit of fixing is still there.

20. **Karma and rebirth hold, with nothing carried across.** Actions bear fruit, and one life conditions the next,
    as one flame lights another. What is refused is a soul that travels on, and also its opposite, a self that is
    wiped out at death. Emptiness doesn't prove rebirth. It only removes the objection that rebirth would need a
    carrier.

21. **Non-dual means "not two", not "all one".** There is no real wall between the knower and the known. But they
    don't melt into one big substance either. Sound stays sound and colour stays colour: close without merging.
    Even the "oneness" can become a new thing to hold.

22. **Awakening is recognising, not producing.** Freedom is not built by effort, and it is not a hidden state
    under the clutter. It is seeing clearly that appearing was never fixed. Practice undoes the habit of fixing, and
    clear understanding is what keeps that recognition steady. It deepens layer by layer and is recognised again
    and again.

23. **"Thusness" is things seen just as they are.** Each moment is complete in itself, with nothing behind it. It
    is not a hidden essence and not raw experience before any thought. It is ordinary: the bell rings, the tea is
    bitter.

24. **Compassion needs no self behind it, and emptiness takes nothing from it.** The teaching itself was given out
    of compassion. Kindness works, and is felt, without a fixed self behind it. Love, sorrow and care stay exactly
    where they were. *Not yet settled:* how compassion relates to awakening.

25. **Buddha nature is not a perfect awareness hidden inside.** It is the empty, knowing clarity of experience,
    recognised rather than dug up. *Not yet settled:* the qualities of a buddha, and what "already a buddha" means.

26. **Effortlessness is what is left when the fixing stops.** It is not a better kind of effort or a state to keep
    up. "Effortless" doesn't mean you can skip the clear seeing that undoes the habit.

27. **Things don't stop existing when no one is thinking of them.** Under anaesthesia no time is felt passing, and
    looked for closely, no thread of time is found. But that doesn't mean the three hours didn't happen: the clock,
    the surgeon and the patient on waking all count them, and that everyday count is valid. "It went on by itself" and
    "it stopped existing" are the two mistakes the Middle Way avoids.

## Part Two: The clear, knowing quality of experience (luminosity)

1. **Clarity is the appearing itself.** It is not a light shining behind things or an awareness looking on. A
   sound is heard as a sound, and that's all.

2. **Clarity is empty too.** If clarity is treated as a glowing substance behind experience, it becomes one more
   foundation. Clarity and emptiness go together, and neither owns the other.

3. **"Unobstructed" doesn't mean "lasting".** Nothing blocks things from showing up. That doesn't mean a stream
   that lasts forever, or a store that things come out of.

4. **Primordial purity: from timeless beginning, purified of inherentness.** There was never a time when things
   existed by themselves. So "purified" doesn't mean cleaned at some point. Nothing was ever there to clean. It
   does not mean a confused life is already free: the path is still needed.

5. **Spontaneous presence is dependence with no producer behind it.** Things appear complete, owing nothing to a
   hidden source. "Spontaneous" never means "uncaused". It is the same appearing as dependent arising, described
   from the side of experience instead of analysis. Neither description is higher.

6. **Thoughts are the show, with nothing behind it.** Thoughts are not dirt on a mirror. They are part of how
   things appear, with no projector behind them.

7. **Knowing itself doesn't need a separate knower.** Self-knowing awareness is not a little witness inside.
   Following Mipham, self-knowing is accepted as an everyday way of speaking, to explain memory, but it is not
   found when looked for, like everything else.

8. **Vividness means nothing in between.** Things feel vivid when the sense of distance drops away. It is not
   extra intensity, and it is not a raw layer found under thought.

9. **The taste is real; turning it into a Self is the mistake.** Experiences of pure awareness or luminous
   presence are genuine. The danger is that awareness quietly becomes the new self, the new ground.

10. **Wisdom completes itself.** Insight and direct knowing are not two faculties. Insight becomes direct knowing
    when it no longer needs an object to look at.

11. **Joining Nāgārjuna and Dzogchen is the author's own reading.** The books say so openly. Some Dzogchen lineages
    see it differently.

12. **Rigpa is knowing the nature of things, not a super-awareness that is their nature.** It means recognising
    appearing as unfixed, dependent and empty. Not recognising this is not a hidden nature going unseen. It is only
    taking things as fixed.

13. **Thoughts free themselves.** A thought that is recognised as unfixed releases itself as it comes, like a
    drawing on water that needs no eraser. Pushing thoughts down only makes a stillness to cling to.

14. **There is no background awareness.** Sounds don't need a listener behind them to be clear. When the witness
    isn't found, experience doesn't go blank: the tree looks more vivid, not less.

15. **The "basis" is not a foundation.** Dzogchen speaks of a basis from which everything unfolds. The books keep the
    word but mean only the emptiness of appearing: nothing that things rest on or come out of, and nothing hidden
    waiting to be uncovered.

---

## The whole view in five lines

- Nothing exists by itself, and nothing is unreal.
- Emptiness is not a thing behind things, and it is empty too.
- The mind, awareness and luminosity get no special exemption.
- Freedom is recognising that appearing was never fixed, not producing a new state.
- Everyday life, kindness and the path all stay fully in place.
